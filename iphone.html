Syslog, pcap, crash reports, and lockdown diagnostics are on iPhone Advanced.

How to trigger sysdiagnose on the iPhone
  1. Trigger sysdiagnose on the phone (Volume Up, Volume Down, Side — ~0.7s), wait ~5 minutes.
  2. Connect USB and request the Apple device in the browser.
  3. Generate or load a pair record (Trust This Computer).
  4. Refresh the archive list, select one, and analyze in the browser.
